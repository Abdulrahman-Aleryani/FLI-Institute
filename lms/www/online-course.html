General English Courses

The English Language Program at FLI provides a complete and structured learning journey designed to develop learners' English skills from beginner to advanced levels. The program helps students build a strong language foundation and improve their ability to communicate confidently in daily, academic, and professional environments. The program is designed for students, professionals, and anyone who wants to learn English or improve their language level through an organised and effective learning system. It focuses on developing speaking, listening, reading, writing, pronunciation, vocabulary, and grammar skills through practical activities and interactive learning. FLI follows an interactive teaching approach with practical conversations, educational activities, continuous assessment, and a complete online learning environment including live classes, assignments, exams, and progress tracking. The program consists of three main stages: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, allowing learners to progress step by step from basic English foundations to advanced communication. Curriculum: American English File

Courses

Beginner Diploma

A foundation stage designed to develop essential English skills and help learners communicate in everyday situations with confidence.

Courses:

  • Access A & B
  • Basic A & B
  • Level 1A & 1B

Intermediate Diploma

A development stage focused on improving fluency, communication skills, vocabulary, and practical use of English.

Courses:

  • Weaving It Together 1
  • Level 2A & 2B
  • Weaving It Together 2
  • Level 3A & 3B
  • Weaving It Together 3

Advanced Diploma

An advanced stage designed to develop professional and academic English skills and prepare learners for higher-level communication and international English requirements.

Courses:

  • Level 4A & 4B
  • Weaving It Together 4A
  • Level 5A & 5B
  • Weaving It Together 4B
  • Level 6A & 6B
  • iBT Preparation

After completing the program, learners will be able to communicate confidently in English, understand different conversations and texts, use vocabulary and grammar accurately, and apply English skills in study, work, and everyday life.
